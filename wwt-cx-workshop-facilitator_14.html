How to run this workshop
  1. Set the client profile. Open Client to record the name, industry, attendees, and — where you have them — the CX maturity level and ROI headline from the other CX tools. Upload the client logo to co-brand the header and readout.
  2. Work the run of show. Move top-to-bottom through the agenda in the left rail. Each module has an objective, facilitator prompts, and fields to capture input as you go.
  3. Prioritize together. In Use Case Prioritization, drag each use case on the value-vs-effort grid. The ranked shortlist and quadrants update live — quick wins surface at the top.
  4. Land the roadmap. Sequence initiatives into Now / Next / Later, then capture agreed actions with owners, decisions, and a parking lot.
  5. Share the readout. Open Readout for an Executive Summary that pulls everything together, then use your browser's print / save-as-PDF to leave it behind. Save session keeps a working file you can re-import later.

Glossary

AI capabilities

AI Virtual Agent
Conversational AI that handles interactions autonomously — understanding intent and resolving inquiries end-to-end without a live agent.
Real-time Agent Assist
AI that listens to live calls and surfaces in-the-moment guidance to the agent: suggested responses, knowledge articles, next-best actions, and compliance prompts.
Call Summarization
AI-generated post-call notes that capture intent, steps taken, outcome, and follow-ups — replacing manual after-call work.
Automated QA
AI scoring of interactions against the quality scorecard (compliance, empathy, script adherence, resolution), extending coverage from a small manual sample toward every contact.
AI Workforce Management
AI forecasting and scheduling that match staffing to demand, reducing paid labor lost to over-scheduling, idle time, and forecast error.
Proactive Churn Prevention
AI that flags customers likely to leave and triggers proactive outreach before they churn — protecting revenue rather than cutting cost.
Agent Onboarding & Coaching
AI-assisted training, simulated practice, and real-time coaching that shorten time-to-proficiency and reduce early attrition.
Deepfake Defense
Voice-biometric and synthetic-voice detection with automated screening that intercepts spam, spoofed, and fraudulent calls before they reach an agent.

CX metrics

CSAT
Customer Satisfaction — a post-interaction rating of how satisfied the customer was.
NPS
Net Promoter Score — likelihood a customer would recommend the brand.
AHT
Average Handle Time — the mean duration of a contact, including talk and after-call work.
FCR
First Contact Resolution — the share of issues resolved on the first interaction, with no follow-up needed.
Containment / Deflection
The share of contacts fully resolved by automation without reaching a live agent.
Cost per contact
The fully loaded cost to handle a single customer interaction.
Shrinkage
Paid agent time not spent handling contacts — breaks, training, idle time, and forecast error.

Prioritization

Value vs. Effort
The two axes used to place use cases: business/customer value against implementation difficulty (data, integration, change, risk).
Quick win
High value, low effort — do these first to prove value fast.
Big bet
High value, high effort — worth a longer, sequenced play.
Fill-in
Low value, low effort — easy but modest impact; do opportunistically.
Time sink
Low value, high effort — generally avoid or defer.
